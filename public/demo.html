Use it alongside YouTube

  1. Download and unzip the extension. In chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, then select Load unpacked.
  2. In the extension popup, enter this site's HTTPS origin and the app token, then select Save connection.
  3. Open a normal English YouTube video, refresh it, enable English CC, and open the companion. Agree to caption processing and start.

Prepared motion follows the video's clock. Missing current motion can pause the video to buffer. No captions means no translation; this version does not capture audio.

Research prototype. Source-pose interpretation, handshapes and ASL meaning remain unverified. This workspace tests the model pipeline; it is not a verified sign-language interpreter.
